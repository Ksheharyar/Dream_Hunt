EMBERBLADE

The village of Holvane has fallen quiet. Your mentor Doras lies slain by a Hollowed creature, and a dying ember-spirit hands you the shattered hilt of an ancient blade. Gather the three Ember Shards scattered across the Cinderwood, the Sunken Mines, and Frostpeak Keep — reforge the Emberblade — and end the corruption at its source: the Hollow King.

Move: Arrows / A-D  |  Jump: Space / Up  |  Sword: Z  |  Flame Bolt: X
Gamepad and touch supported.
